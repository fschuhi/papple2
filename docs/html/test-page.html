A listing

The end of LOAD_LEVEL, from 6267 to its RTS at 62c3, laid out the way edit() shows it: address, bytes, the label column, the instruction cut at 32 characters, the comment. The label column is empty here; the routine's only label, LOAD_LEVEL, is at 6238.

Two loops: the inner one goes through the 28 columns of one row of the level, the outer one through the 16 rows. The arrows are the jumps the run took inside the excerpt, the ones edit() draws with +-- and +->. The number is how often the run took the jump; edit() doesn't show it.
