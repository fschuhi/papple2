## Explaining `papple2`

- A diagram: how a byte reaches the screen, and what the emulated Apple II is. `Apple2` builds `Display`, `Speaker`, `SoftSwitches`, `Memory` and `CPU`. A store such as `STA $2000` goes through `Memory.write_data()` and `write_byte()` to `Display.update()`, which draws into the pygame surface; the soft switches set the display mode; `window.present()` shows the frame every 1000 passes. Three modules take part (`memory.py`, `apple.py`, `window.py`). The hi-res renderer of 2026-10-11 redraws a written byte together with both neighbours, since colours smear across byte boundaries.
